\section*{Data and Code Availability}

The CADE adjudication records used as ground truth are public
and accessible through the
\textit{Conselho Administrativo de Defesa Econ\^omica} portal
(\url{https://www.gov.br/cade}). The S\~ao Paulo Bolsa
Eletr\^onica de Compras (BEC) data are administrative records
maintained by the State of S\~ao Paulo Treasury and were
obtained through institutional channels. The replication
archive containing the R, Python, and \LaTeX{} source files,
the construction scripts for all derived datasets, and the
sequence of commits that produced the analysis is available
upon publication via the journal's data-availability portal.
The archive includes: (i) the canonical pipeline (\texttt{00\_master.R}
plus the build, clean, and analysis scripts) that regenerates
all tables and figures from the four primary parquet files
(\texttt{firm\_loss\_stats.parquet}, \texttt{firm\_tender\_map.parquet},
\texttt{FREQ\_PARTICIP\_rebuilt.parquet}, \texttt{LOSERS\_rebuilt.parquet});
(ii) the institutional-identification scripts producing
Tables~\ref{tab:modal_id} and~\ref{tab:mccrary}; (iii) the
cross-sector replication script producing
Table~\ref{tab:cross_sector}; and (iv) the inverse-CNAE
mapping diagnostic discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:cross_sector}.

\section*{Numerical Reproducibility Note}

The numerical claims in this paper were generated by a single
pipeline run on the canonical 2009--2019 BEC parquet files
plus the CADE crossmatch CSV. The pipeline applies a strict
14-character CNPJ zero-padding convention before any merge,
which is required to recover the full 193 FL--CADE
co-bidder set; alternative padding conventions yield the
$98$-firm subset reported in earlier draft tables, and we flag
the convention here so future replications can reproduce
either count deterministically. The headline AUC of $0.94$
corresponds to point estimate $0.9389$ on $193$ positives among
$16{,}843$ always-losers; the $95\%$ confidence interval
$[0.932, 0.946]$ uses DeLong's method, with stratified
percentile and BCa bootstrap intervals ($B = 5{,}000$ and
$2{,}000$, respectively) agreeing to three decimal places. The
cross-sector replication (\S\ref{sec:cross_sector}) was
executed on the same canonical sample with the BEC item-code
2-digit prefix as the sector partition; the inverse CNAE
mapping (\S\ref{sec:cross_sector} footnote) used the
\texttt{Firms\_final.parquet} CNAE codes recovered for $34$ of
the $47$ BEC-active CADE-defendants. The full sequence of
commits that produced the final tables and figures is
documented in the replication archive's git log.
