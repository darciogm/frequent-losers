\item \textit{Notes:} Score: $\log(1+\text{tenders\_count})$, ranked

draws on participation occurring after CADE adjudications were already
underway for some cartels. All operational claims in the paper are
read off the temporal-holdout block. The screening statistic
requires only contract-award records (winner identity, participant
identity, item identifier); no bid microdata.
