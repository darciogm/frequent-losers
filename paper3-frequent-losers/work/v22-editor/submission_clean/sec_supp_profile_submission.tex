\section{Economic Profile and Robustness: Full Grids}
\label{supp:profile}

This section holds the full grids behind the economic-profile and
robustness diagnostics of the submitted Appendix~D. Group definitions,
the standardized-difference method, and the headline reads are in that
appendix. ``Cobidder'' is an adjudication-anchored exposure label
throughout---a firm that co-bid with a firm named in a CADE
proceeding---and carries no inference of participation in any agreement.

\subsection{Comparison Groups}
\label{supp:profile_groups}

All groups are drawn from the always-loser universe (firms with zero
recorded wins over the calibration window, $N=16{,}843$). The
adjudication-anchored validation target is the broad set of
$\valMainCobidders$ always-loser cobidders, of which $\valMainCobFL$
($52.4\%$) satisfy the frequent-loser cut and $\valMainCobNonFL$
($47.6\%$) do not, so the label is not frequent-loser-conditioned. The
$\valDirectCADE$ direct CADE defendants are \emph{winners} and are
excluded from every cobidder group; the $6$ that appear in the
always-loser universe are excluded from the profile contrasts. The
profile contrast is run \emph{within} the frequent-loser stratum---group
D (FL non-cobidders, $N=2{,}394$) versus group E (FL cobidders,
$N=341$). The complete group register, including the home-market and
placebo groups used in the robustness families below, follows the same
construction.

\subsection{Full Standardized-Difference Table}
\label{supp:profile_smd_full}

The top-ranked standardized differences (Cohen's $d$, E minus D) appear
in the submitted Appendix~D. The full variable table extends panels
(A)--(E): participation and survival, breadth and concentration,
defendant-proximity / opportunity, modality-quorum proxies, and timing.
The largest gaps are the defendant-proximity variables (panel C:
$n\_cases$ $d=6.17$, $n\_def\_firms$ $d=4.27$), which are partly
mechanical because the cobidder label is itself adjudication-anchored;
holding exposure fixed (regression on $\log E_i^{\mathrm{MED}}$, a binned
score term, breadth, and active years, and CEM on $E_i^{\mathrm{MED}}$
deciles) collapses the item-group HHI gap from $d\approx 0.13$ to
$\approx 0$ and the breadth and persistence gaps toward zero. Only the
defendant-contact variables survive, and that survival is partly
mechanical.

\subsection{Robustness: Threshold Sweep, Bunching, Markets, Null Controls}
\label{supp:profile_robustness}

\paragraph{Monotonicity.} Across participation bins, cobidder prevalence
rises (Spearman $\rho=\valProfMonoPrevRho$) while mean excess contact
shows no rising pattern ($\rho=\valProfMonoExcessRho$): the score ranks
firms by \emph{exposure}, not collusion intensity.

\paragraph{Threshold sweep and bunching.} The FL14 cut $T_i\geq 14$
equals $\mathrm{median}+1.5\times\mathrm{IQR}$ ($=13.5$, rounded up) of
the always-loser participation distribution; it is an
\emph{administrative} construction, computed ex post and \emph{never
published}. Discrimination changes smoothly across a placebo-threshold
grid with no discontinuity at $14$, and the participation distribution
declines smoothly through the cut (local density ratio
$\valProfBunchRatio$, indistinguishable from $1$), so there is no
strategic bunching. The full placebo-threshold grid and the bunching
histogram are reported here.

\paragraph{Market-specific zero-win.} Four home-market loser definitions
overlap the global set on only $0.33$--$0.55$ of their losers; the
global definition is not dominated by any of them (global ROC-AUC
$\valProfNegCtrlRealAUC$ vs.\ the best home-market alternative
$\valProfMarketZeroWinBest$) and is stable to leave-one-item-group-out
re-estimation.

\paragraph{Negative controls.} Against matched null targets ($B=500$
draws, seed \texttt{20260603}), the real-anchor ROC-AUC
($\valProfNegCtrlRealAUC$) is statistically indistinguishable from the
matched placebo-anchor distribution (mean $\valProfNegCtrlPlaceboAUC$;
empirical $p=0.46$), and anchors built from non-CADE high-volume winners
generate raw discrimination at least as high (null mean $0.78$;
$p=0.91$). The negative controls corroborate the opportunity
decomposition rather than rescue the screen: the raw ranking reflects
generic co-participation arithmetic, not a signature specific to the
adjudicated defendant network.
