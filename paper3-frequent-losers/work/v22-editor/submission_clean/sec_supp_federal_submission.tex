\section{Federal Stress Test (ComprasNet): Full Construction and Battery}
\label{supp:federal}

This section deposits the full federal construction---label funnel,
opportunity-cell construction, and the complete control-function, timing,
leave-one-case-out, negative-control, and armor batteries---behind the
compact federal stress-test summary in Appendix~G
(\ref{app:federal_audit_submission}). The federal panel is a stress test of
how far forensic priority \emph{ports} across platforms, not an independent
validation. ``CADE-defendant contact'' is an adjudication-anchored
\emph{exposure} label---a firm shares a tender-item with a directly
adjudicated CADE defendant---and never membership or liability. The legal
anchors are the same numbered CADE cases used on BEC, \emph{partially
overlapping} with the BEC anchors by construction
(Section~\ref{sec:comparative}). The federal panel has no sealed-bid
modality, so no modality stratification is reported.

\subsection{Label-Construction Funnel}
\label{supp:federal_funnel}

Direct CADE defendants are matched to federal participant records by the
canonical establishment-anchored CNPJ rule; their defendant tender-items
locate the adjudicated environments; unique always-loser firms sharing at
least one federal tender-item with an anchor define the broad cobidder
label. Direct defendants are excluded and the frequent-loser flag is never
used to build the label.

\subsection{Opportunity-Cell Construction and Expected Contact}
\label{supp:federal_opp_cells}

A firm can appear near a CADE anchor simply because it operates in
environments where defendants are active; the opportunity cells discipline
this exposure before any score is credited. We partition the federal award
layer into procurement-opportunity cells $g$ using three nested
definitions: \textbf{COARSE} $=$ year (the item-group margin is not
observed federally---federal item codes are buyer-embedded rather than
drawn from a shared classification,
Section~\ref{sec:comparative_observability}); \textbf{MEDIUM} $=$ year
$\times$ buyer (UASG)---the reference definition for the headline federal
within-stratum results; and \textbf{STRICT} $=$ item code $\times$ year
$\times$ buyer. Modality is not a margin (the federal panel is pure
Preg\~ao). For firm $i$, with $n_{ig}$ the tender-items held in cell $g$
and $p_g$ the cell defendant-contact rate computed leave-one-out, expected
contact is $E_i=\sum_g n_{ig}\,p_g$, observed contact is $O_i$, and excess
is $X_i=O_i-E_i$. The mean cell defendant-contact rate $\bar p_g$ is around
$\valFedAudPbarRange$ across the three definitions; finer cells gain
resolution at the cost of support (under STRICT,
$\valFedAudStrictSparseShare$ of cells hold fewer than five tender-items).

\subsection{Power Bounding and Matched Permutation}
\label{supp:federal_power_permutation}

A positive-control score ($O_i$ itself) ranks the label within the
\emph{same} strata to confirm the design recovers a residual when one
exists; a calibration injects synthetic within-stratum signal to size the
permutation test; and the label is shuffled only within matched
exposure-by-participation strata. The within-stratum positive control
reaches $\valFedAudOiControlMedium$, confirming the design has power. The
permutation test is correctly sized under the null and rejects with
probability $\valFedAudPowerFive$ once the true within-stratum AUC reaches
$0.55$ (probability $\valFedAudPowerTen$ at $0.60$). Under the
matched-strata permutation the observed PR-AUC \valFedAudPermVerdict\
($p=\valFedAudPermCp$); the within-stratum AUC across cell granularities
ranges $\valFedAudWithinCoarse$ (coarse) to $\valFedAudWithinStrict$
(strict).

\subsection{Timing, Leakage, and Label-Frozen Prospective Test}
\label{supp:federal_timing}

We freeze the score, the always-loser status, and the frequent-loser
threshold to the $2013$--$2016$ window and evaluate against $2017$--$2019$
labels, on the realistic full candidate universe (entrants carried at score
zero). A fully label-frozen variant additionally freezes the label
ingredients---always-loser status from $2013$--$2016$ wins only and
defendant contact from $2013$--$2016$ tender-items only---and ranks
\emph{new} defendant contact arising in $2017$--$2019$.

\subsection{Case Leave-One-Case-Out (LOCO) Concentration}
\label{supp:federal_loco}

The largest federal case supplies $\valFedAudTopCaseShare$ of all positives
and $\valFedAudTopCaseTP$ of the top-$500$ true positives. Dropping it cuts
PR-AUC from $\valFedAudLOCOfullPRAUC$ to $\valFedAudLOCOdropLargestPRAUC$
($\valFedAudLOCOdropPct$); dropping the top two cases leaves PR-AUC
$\valFedAudLOCOdropTwoPRAUC$. A clustered randomization-inference test
(buyer$\times$year cells, $B=1{,}000$) shows the pooled ordering is
\valFedAudClusterRIverdict\ ($p\valFedAudClusterRIp$) while case-coverage
breadth is \valFedAudClusterRIcovVerdict\ ($p=\valFedAudClusterRIcovP$).

\subsection{Negative Controls}
\label{supp:federal_negative_controls}

The same award-layer ordering is re-evaluated against (i) placebo
anchors---random federal firms matched on participation volume to the CADE
defendants---and (ii) non-CADE high-volume-winner anchors. If the ordering
reproduces under these placebos, it is not cartel-specific. Against placebo
anchors the score retains AUC $\valFedAudPlaceboAUC$, and against non-CADE
high-volume-winner anchors $\valFedAudHVWinnerAUC$, versus $\valFedAudRawAUC$
against the CADE anchors---neither placebo is rejected (empirical
$p=0.258$ and $p=0.582$ respectively), placing the surviving ordering in
the realm of \valFedAudNegControlVerdict.

\subsection{Armor Pack: Robustness of the Federal Verdict}
\label{supp:federal_armor}

The breadth leg of the BEC armor (restricting positives to firms sharing at
least two defendant tender-items) is not produced federally---it rests on a
single federal run, so it is recorded in the replication notes rather than
promoted to a verdict. The firm-level leave-one-out $E_i$ benchmark reaches
$\valFedAudExpLOO$; recomputing each cell's density from non-cobidder rows
only leaves a label-blind opportunity ranking at $\valFedAudExpLB$. The
within-stratum AUC is stable across cell granularities
($\valFedAudWithinCoarse$ coarse to $\valFedAudWithinStrict$ strict).
