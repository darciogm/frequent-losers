\section*{Appendix Roadmap}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix Roadmap}
\label{app:guide_submission}

The appendix builds from definitions to validation.
\ref{app:data_labels_submission} fixes the objects: the award-record
universe, the CADE--BEC matching rules, the $\valMainCobidders$-firm
adjudication-anchored cobidder label, and the funnel that reconciles every
sample count. \ref{app:framework_submission} develops the reduced-form
ranking model behind the screen---the monotone-likelihood-ratio result
that orders firms by loss intensity, the over-crediting logic that flows
from it, and the enforcer's optimal-stopping rule that prices how far down
the ranking to descend. \ref{app:validation_audits_submission} is the core
of the validation, where the opportunity-adjusted, timing, leakage, and
case-composition audits discipline what the ranking can and cannot mean.
\ref{app:profile_submission} characterizes the flagged firms and reports
the compact score diagnostics; \ref{app:bid_benchmark_submission} adds the
bid-layer benchmark and traces the cost--recall frontier of sequential
award-to-bid gatekeeping. \ref{app:scope_adaptation_submission} reports the
price-scope evidence and the adaptive-use diagnostics, and
\ref{app:federal_audit_submission} re-runs the validation battery on the
federal ComprasNet panel as an out-of-platform stress test. Full grids,
permutation draws, threshold sweeps, the bid-feature dictionary, the long
proofs, and the supporting figures appear in the Online Supplement.

One convention governs the whole appendix. The ranking is a statement
about \emph{forensic priority}, not a finding of conduct or cartel
membership; the validation labels are adjudication-anchored
\emph{exposure} measures, not legal findings; and the price evidence
describes \emph{scope}, not damages or a causal price effect. We restate
this boundary only where a result could otherwise be misread.
