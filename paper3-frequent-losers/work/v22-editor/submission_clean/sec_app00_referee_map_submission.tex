\section*{Appendix Roadmap}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix Roadmap}
\label{app:guide_submission}

The appendix follows the logic of the evidence-allocation design and
builds from definitions to validation. \ref{app:data_labels_submission}
fixes the objects: the award-record universe, the CADE--BEC matching
rules, the distinction between direct defendants and adjudication-anchored
cobidders, and the label funnel that reconciles every sample count.
\ref{app:framework_submission} develops the reduced-form ranking model
behind the screen---the monotone-likelihood-ratio result, the exit-margin
logic that gives the participation count its informational content, and
the platform-survival audit that tests it descriptively.
\ref{app:validation_audits_submission} is the core of the validation: the
opportunity-adjusted, timing, leakage, and case-composition audits that
discipline what the ranking can and cannot be read to mean.
\ref{app:profile_submission} characterizes the flagged firms and reports
the score diagnostics; \ref{app:bid_benchmark_submission} and
\ref{app:forensic_sequence_submission} turn to the bid-layer benchmark and
trace the cost-recall frontier of sequential award-to-bid gatekeeping. The
price-scope evidence and the adaptive-use diagnostics close the sequence in
\ref{app:scope_adaptation_submission} and
\ref{app:strategic_adaptation_submission}; \ref{app:federal_audit_submission}
re-runs the validation battery on the federal ComprasNet panel. Full
robustness grids, permutation draws, threshold sweeps, the bid-feature
dictionary, and the supporting figures are deposited in the Online
Supplement, whose manifest maps each item to the script that generates it.

One reading convention governs the whole appendix. The ranking is a
statement about \emph{forensic priority}---where scarce investigative
attention is most likely to pay off---not a finding of conduct or cartel
membership; the validation labels are adjudication-anchored \emph{exposure}
measures, not legal findings; and the price evidence describes \emph{scope}
(where flagged environments sit in the price distribution), not damages or
a causal price effect. We restate this boundary only where a specific
result could otherwise be misread.
