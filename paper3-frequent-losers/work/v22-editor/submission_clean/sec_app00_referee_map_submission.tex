\section*{Appendix Roadmap}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix Roadmap}
\label{app:guide_submission}

This appendix supplies technical support for the main text and is
organized around the evidentiary burden of an evidence-allocation
design. \ref{app:data_labels_submission} documents the
award-record universe, the CADE--BEC matching rules, the
direct-defendant versus adjudication-anchored cobidder distinction,
and the label funnel and sample reconciliation. \ref{app:framework_submission}
gives the reduced-form ranking framework, the monotone-likelihood-ratio
result, the exit-margin logic, and the platform-survival audit.
\ref{app:validation_audits_submission} reports the
opportunity-adjusted, timing, leakage, and case-composition audits
that discipline alternative interpretations of the ranking.
\ref{app:profile_submission} summarizes the economic-profile
and score diagnostics. \ref{app:bid_benchmark_submission}
documents the bid-layer benchmark and the cost-recall frontier for
sequential award$\rightarrow$bid gatekeeping. \ref{app:scope_adaptation_submission}
reports the price-scope evidence and the adaptive-deployment protocol.
Full robustness grids, permutation draws, threshold sweeps, the
bid-feature dictionary, and additional diagnostic figures are
collected in the Online Supplement, whose manifest maps each item to
its generating script. Throughout, the appendix interprets the
ranking as forensic priority, not proof of conduct or cartel
membership, and treats price evidence as scope information rather
than damages.
