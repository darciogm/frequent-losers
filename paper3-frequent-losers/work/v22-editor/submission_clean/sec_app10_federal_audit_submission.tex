\section{Federal Audit Battery (ComprasNet)}
\label{app:federal_audit_submission}

This appendix re-runs the full BEC validation battery of
Appendix~\ref{app:validation_audits_submission} on the federal ComprasNet
panel ($2013$--$2019$), stage for stage, under the same definitions.
It is the audit-side companion to the comparative reading in
Section~\ref{sec:comparative} and supplies the federal column of
Table~\ref{tab:comparative_audit}. Throughout, ``CADE-defendant
contact'' denotes an \emph{adjudication-anchored exposure} label: a
firm shares a tender-item with a directly adjudicated CADE
defendant. It does not assert cartel membership, agreement, or
liability. The federal panel has no sealed-bid modality, so no
modality stratification is reported; the legal anchors are the same
numbered CADE cases used on BEC, partially overlapping by
construction (Section~\ref{sec:comparative}). The audits read the
portability of forensic priority, not membership, damages, or proof.

\subsection{Opportunity-Cell Construction and Expected Contact}
\label{app:federal_opp_cells}

\textit{Purpose.} A firm can appear near a CADE anchor simply
because it operates in environments where defendants are active; the
opportunity cells discipline this exposure before any score is
credited, exactly as in Appendix~\ref{app:validation_audits_submission}.

\textit{Method.} We partition the federal award layer into
procurement-opportunity cells $g$ using three nested definitions,
adapted to federal observability:
\textbf{COARSE} $=$ year (the item-group margin is not observed
federally---federal item codes are buyer-embedded rather than drawn
from a shared classification, Section~\ref{sec:comparative_observability});
\textbf{MEDIUM} $=$ year $\times$ buyer (UASG)---the reference
definition for the headline federal within-stratum results; and
\textbf{STRICT} $=$ item code $\times$ year $\times$ buyer. Modality
is not a margin (the federal panel is pure Preg\~ao). For firm $i$,
with $n_{ig}$ the tender-items held in cell $g$ and $p_g$ the cell
defendant-contact rate computed leave-one-out, expected contact is
$E_i=\sum_g n_{ig}\,p_g$, observed contact is $O_i$, and excess is
$X_i=O_i-E_i$.

\textit{Result.} The mean cell defendant-contact rate
$\bar p_g$ is around $\valFedAudPbarRange$ across the three definitions;
finer cells gain resolution at the cost of support (under STRICT,
$\valFedAudStrictSparseShare$ of cells hold fewer than five
tender-items). The cell-construction grids are in the federal supplement.

\subsection{Power Bounding and Matched Permutation}
\label{app:federal_power_permutation}

\textit{Purpose.} Establish that a within-stratum null is
informative rather than underpowered, and that the matched-strata
permutation is correctly sized, mirroring the BEC armor pack.

\textit{Method.} A positive-control score ($O_i$ itself) ranks the
label within the \emph{same} strata to confirm the design recovers a
residual when one exists; a calibration injects synthetic
within-stratum signal to size the permutation test; and the label is
shuffled only within matched exposure-by-participation strata.

\textit{Result.} The within-stratum positive control
reaches $\valFedAudOiControlMedium$, confirming the design has power.
The permutation test is correctly sized under the null and rejects
with probability $\valFedAudPowerFive$ once the true within-stratum
AUC reaches $0.55$ (probability $\valFedAudPowerTen$ at $0.60$). Under
the matched-strata permutation the observed PR-AUC
$\valFedAudPermVerdict$ ($p=\valFedAudPermCp$); the within-stratum AUC
across cell granularities ranges $\valFedAudWithinCoarse$ (coarse) to
$\valFedAudWithinStrict$ (strict).

\subsection{Case Leave-One-Case-Out (LOCO) Concentration}
\label{app:federal_loco}

\textit{Purpose.} Test whether the raw federal ranking is carried by
a single adjudicated case, mirroring the BEC leave-one-case-out
audit.

\textit{Result.} The largest federal case supplies
$\valFedAudTopCaseShare$ of all positives and $\valFedAudTopCaseTP$ of
the top-$500$ true positives. Dropping it cuts PR-AUC from
$\valFedAudLOCOfullPRAUC$ to $\valFedAudLOCOdropLargestPRAUC$
($\valFedAudLOCOdropPct$); dropping the top two cases leaves PR-AUC
$\valFedAudLOCOdropTwoPRAUC$. A clustered randomization-inference test
(buyer$\times$year cells, $B=1{,}000$) shows the pooled ordering is
$\valFedAudClusterRIverdict$ ($p=\valFedAudClusterRIp$) while
case-coverage breadth is $\valFedAudClusterRIcovVerdict$
($p=\valFedAudClusterRIcovP$). The leave-one-case-out distribution is
in the federal supplement.

\subsection{Negative Controls}
\label{app:federal_negative_controls}

\textit{Purpose.} Establish whether any surviving federal ordering
is cartel-specific or generic co-participation geometry, mirroring
the negative-control logic of Section~\ref{sec:cobidder_type}.

\textit{Method.} The same award-layer ordering is re-evaluated
against (i) placebo anchors---random federal firms matched on
participation volume to the CADE defendants---and (ii) non-CADE
high-volume-winner anchors. If the ordering reproduces under these
placebos, it is not cartel-specific.

\textit{Result.} Against placebo anchors the score retains
AUC $\valFedAudPlaceboAUC$, and against non-CADE high-volume-winner
anchors $\valFedAudHVWinnerAUC$, versus $\valFedAudRawAUC$ against the
CADE anchors---placing the surviving ordering in the realm of
$\valFedAudNegControlVerdict$.

\subsection{Graduation: What Ports and What Deflates}
\label{app:federal_graduation}

for each stage of the audit, what \emph{ports} from BEC, what
\emph{deflates} under federal discipline, and where the structural
boundary holds on both platforms. The federal CADE anchors are the
same numbered adjudicated processes used on BEC, recovered through a
multi-pass establishment-anchored linkage (BEC-side crossmatch reuse,
then RAIS employment-link audit, then a Receita Federal \emph{Empresas}
open-dump pass), and are \emph{partially overlapping} with the BEC
anchors by construction (Section~\ref{sec:comparative}); the table below reports

\paragraph{The structural boundary holds on both platforms.}
The one positive cross-platform statement the graduation supports is a
\emph{negative} about the screen's reach, and it is on-thesis. On the
federal panel the firm-level AUC of the loser-side score against
\emph{direct} CADE defendants is $\valFedAudBoundaryAUC$ on the full
candidate universe and $\valFedAudBoundaryAUCALpool$ within the
always-loser pool (point estimates; no CI band was produced for the
federal scope check), consistent with the BEC boundary result of
$\valAUCdirectCADE$. The reason is the same on both platforms: direct
defendants in these adjudications are predominantly winners
($\valFedAudDirectsWinshare$ median win-share across the
$\valFedAudDirectsN$ federal direct-defendant firms), and a loser-side
construct by design does not rank winners. The score concentrates
loser-side exposure and declines to rank the very firms a naive reader
might expect it to flag; that boundary is a feature of the construct,
and it ports.

\paragraph{Reading the graduation.}
The federal battery does not return a degraded-but-positive
deployable ranking, and the appendix does not claim one. What ports is
the \emph{audit}: the raw award-layer concentration looks informative
until opportunity exposure is held fixed, after which the within-stratum
residual is read directly off Table~\ref{tab:federal_graduation}, and
the prospective ranking behaves as it does on BEC. The deliverable an
agency carries across platforms is therefore the decomposition that
tells it how far a cheap award-layer statistic can be trusted before
costly bid-layer work begins---and on the federal system that lesson
binds harder, because the bid-layer step cannot be constructed from
public data at all (Section~\ref{sec:comparative_observability}).
