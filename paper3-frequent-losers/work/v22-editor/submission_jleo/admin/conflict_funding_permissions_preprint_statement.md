`HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED` — Confirm whether any preprint, working-paper,

**Not applicable.** The study uses administrative procurement records and public
adjudication rulings; there are **no human subjects** and no IRB review is
implicated. (No IRB facts are claimed beyond non-applicability.)
