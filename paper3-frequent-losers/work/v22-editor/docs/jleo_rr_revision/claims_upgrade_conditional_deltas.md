# 5. DO NOT TOUCH — claims that must NOT be upgraded regardless of federal results

These are load-bearing disciplinary anchors. Federal results — in either
direction — change none of them. Any edit touching the items below is a
regression and must be reverted.

1. **The 0.49 direct-defendant boundary.** The screen is silent about direct
   CADE defendants (AUC ≈ chance against direct defendants). This is the design,
   demonstrated by D4, and front-paged in intro (lines 145–148) and conclusion
   (line 36, "does not behave like a generic direct-defendant detector"). The
   federal section explicitly does NOT rank defendants on either platform
   (comparative table note; appendix G label definition). **No federal number may
   be used to soften, qualify, or "improve on" this boundary.** Even under
   RESIDUAL-SURVIVES, the residual is over the cobidder exposure target, never
   over direct defendants.

2. **The not-proof-of-membership disclaimers.** Every statement that the screen
   "creates forensic priority, not proof", "indicates where legal attention
   should start, not where legal responsibility ends", and that the target is
   "adjudication-anchored exposure, not cartel membership" (conclusion lines
   56–59, 76–79; intro lines 86–90; cover letter lines 5, 13). The federal
   evidence is anchored on the SAME (partially overlapping) cases, so it can add
   *nothing* to the legal-membership question. RESIDUAL-SURVIVES upgrades the
   protocol's reach, NOT the legal status of any firm.

3. **The C1 organizational result as the headline payload.** "The contribution
   is the organizational result---the award-to-bid recovery decision is a
   sequential cost-recall problem whose frontier, not any cutoff, is the design
   object" (abstract lines 31–34; intro first-contribution paragraph lines
   171–187). C1 is the law-economics-organization core and the JLEO fit. The
   federal section is a *second-platform demonstration of the C2 protocol*; it
   must NOT be promoted into a co-headline that displaces C1, and the frontier
   itself is NOT recomputed federally as a deployment claim (no strict-timing
   bid rerank; bid layer absent federally — see comparative §observability).

4. **"Comparable, not dominant" Imhof framing.** The award layer is *comparable*
   to the bid-moment benchmark and combines only *conditionally* (intro lines
   151–169; conclusion lines 45–54). Never "outperforms." The federal platform
   has no bid layer, so it offers NO new horse-race evidence; do not let a
   federal result revive any dominance reading.

5. **The case-concentration / single-case caveat.** "The apparent reach is
   concentrated in one case" (intro lines 209–211; conclusion lines 31–33). The
   federal LOCO row reports its own case concentration; it does not retroactively
   weaken the BEC single-case caveat.

6. **The retrospective / incumbent-bound scope of the estimated ranking.**
   "Strict prospective ranking fails outside incumbent pools" (intro lines
   142–143; conclusion lines 33–35). The federal label-frozen timing row is its
   own test on its own platform; it does not convert the BEC ranking into a
   prospective device.

7. **The "partially overlapping legal anchors" honesty itself.** This caveat is
   NOT optional polish that can be dropped if federal results look strong. It is
   a permanent factual constraint (same 7 CADE cases anchor both platforms). It
   must survive every editorial pass in every section that cites the federal
   evidence. Removing it to claim "independent replication" is the single most
   damaging regression possible and would be a false claim.

# 6. Tensions flagged between current claim set and planned upgrade (honest)

**T1 — "We do not claim a validated, transferable framework" (intro C2, line
198) directly collides with the doc-99 upgrade "audit protocol demonstrated on
two platforms."** A second-platform demonstration *is* a (partial) validation of
transferability of the framework. The proposed delta resolves this by narrowing
the denied object from "framework" to "score/ranking": the *protocol* is now
shown to travel (that is the upgrade), while the *estimated ranking* remains
non-transferable (unchanged C3). This is internally consistent with the existing
third-contribution sentence (lines 204–214), which already says the *score* is
not portable while the *principle* transfers. **Action: the word-level change
"framework"→"score" in line 198 is mandatory under either variant; without it the
intro contradicts the new section.**

**T2 — Partial anchor overlap caps how strong RESIDUAL-SURVIVES can be sold.**
Because both platforms are anchored on the SAME 7 CADE cases, a surviving federal
residual is NOT independent corroboration of the construct's discriminating
power — it could reflect the same firms/cases reappearing federally. The
RESIDUAL-SURVIVES variants therefore deliberately frame the survival as
*institution-coupled scope information* (D2-style), never as "the signal
replicates independently." This is honest but means RESIDUAL-SURVIVES is a
*weaker* upgrade than a naive reading of doc 99 §9 ("a positive result")
suggests. The lead should not let the cover letter or abstract over-sell it.

**T3 — Abstract length pressure.** The current abstract is ~165 words; the JLEO
cap used here is ≤150. Inserting any cross-platform clause forces a trim. Both
proposed abstracts achieve this by compressing the existing
"prospective-ranking-fails-outside-incumbent-pools" clause (it survives in intro
+ conclusion, so the abstract can carry the shorter form). Flagged so the lead
knows a real sentence was shortened, not just appended. If JLEO's actual cap is
the 75-word editorial abstract in doc 69, a separate compression is needed — out
of scope here; this targets the 150-word manuscript abstract.

**T4 — C1 displacement risk.** doc 99 §4 phrases the upgrade as "C2 →
demonstrated on two platforms," which is C2-local. But a vivid new §5 plus
abstract/intro/conclusion edits can visually elevate the cross-platform story
above C1 (the organizational frontier result). All proposed deltas keep the
cross-platform clause *subordinate* to the C1 organizational sentence (it is
appended after, never before, the "organizational result" clause). Flagged so
this ordering is preserved during integration.

**T5 — Federal frontier is not a deployment claim.** The cost-recall frontier
(C1's concrete object) cannot be recomputed federally as a prospective deployment
because the federal platform has no bid layer (comparative §observability). So
the "two platforms" upgrade is genuinely about the *audit protocol* (C2), not
about C1's frontier traveling. Abstract/intro deltas must say "audit protocol
demonstrated across two platforms," NOT "frontier demonstrated across two
platforms." All proposed wording above respects this; flagged because it is an
easy slip during copy-edit.
