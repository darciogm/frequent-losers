## Operational notes
- **File-format discrepancy:** OUP/Editorial Express accept PDF/Word/RTF. To be safe, upload the **manuscript as PDF** and keep LaTeX source + a Word/RTF-ready fallback. No PDF-only restriction observed (recorded; if the live portal shows PDF-only at submission time, follow the stricter rule).
- **Double-spacing + 1.25" margins:** current manuscript compiles at `\baselinestretch 1.48` with 28–30 mm margins. A **double-spaced, 1.25"-margin submission build** must be generated for upload (see package step). This is the one binding formatting gap.
