\title{Online Appendix to:\\
Sequencing Enforcement Under Incomplete Observability:\\
An Award-Layer Triage Stage for Cartel Detection}

\author[insper]{Darcio Genicolo-Martins\corref{cor1}}
\ead{darciogm1@insper.edu.br}
\author[insper]{Paulo Furquim de Azevedo}
\affiliation[insper]{organization={INSPER},
  city={S\~ao Paulo}, country={Brazil}}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author.}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent This online appendix accompanies \emph{Sequencing
Enforcement Under Incomplete Observability: An Award-Layer
Triage Stage for Cartel Detection} and contains: (A) the
cover-bidding framework, assumptions A1--A6, and proofs of
Lemma~\ref{lem:wins_zero_stmt} and Propositions~\ref{prop:exposure_stmt}--\ref{prop:screening_value}
invoked in the main text; (B) core supporting tables and
figures; (C) identification audits (overlap-restricted
estimates, sign-reversal decomposition, Oster bounds, and the
anti-leakage audit); (D) threshold and specification robustness
tables; (E) operational metrics and detector comparison; and
(F) the staggered-design estimators that return null at the
available bandwidths. References to sections, equations, and
tables in the main text use the same labels as the published
manuscript.
\end{abstract}

\end{frontmatter}
