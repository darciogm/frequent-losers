\noindent\emph{Frequent Losers: A Participation-Based Screen for Public Procurement}\\
Darcio Genicolo-Martins \& Paulo Furquim de Azevedo (Insper)

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item Loser-side participation patterns flag cartel-adjacent procurement environments using award records alone.
  \item Frequent-loser presence associates with 3.6--7.7\% higher prices in the broad sample.
  \item The conditional association reverses sign under strict-overlap matching, indicating selection scope matters.
  \item Against CADE's pre-2020 cartel records, the screen reaches AUC 0.748 on cobidder labels.
  \item The price imprint shrinks 12.6$\times$ from smallest to largest buyers, consistent with thinner institutional capacity.
\end{itemize}
