\noindent\emph{Frequent Losers as a Cartel Screen:\\
Detecting Cover Bidding Without Bid Microdata}\\
Darcio Genicolo-Martins \& Paulo Furquim de Azevedo (Insper)

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item A participation-only cartel screen runs on contract-award records, with no bid microdata.
  \item Frequent losers concentrate where conditional prices are 3.6--7.7\% higher across estimators.
  \item Smallest buyers show a 12.6$\times$ gap relative to the largest---the strongest heterogeneity.
  \item The construct discriminates CADE-cobidder firms at AUC 0.864 under temporal holdout.
  \item A three-stage administrative pathway (Screen, Triage, Forensic) integrates with bid-level tools.
\end{itemize}
