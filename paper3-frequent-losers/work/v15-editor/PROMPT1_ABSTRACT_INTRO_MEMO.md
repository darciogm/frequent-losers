## Remaining concerns in the opening pages

1. **AUC numbers in three formats inside the second-finding sentence** ($\valAUCFLfirm$, $\valAUCFLfirmTemp$, $\valAUCprePost$). The reader meets in-sample, temporal-holdout, and contemporaneous figures in one breath. I considered dropping in-sample to leave only the two operationally honest numbers, but the in-sample number is the reference point against which the leakage audit (§9) is read. Acceptable as-is; if a referee complains, drop in-sample and pick up the audit later.

2. **The "Scope" paragraph could lose the forward reference to §\ref{sec:rob\_imhof}.** It survives because it preempts the reflex that "this should be benchmarked against Imhof et al." In a tighter pass we could move it to §6.

3. **The buyer-size gradient sentence still ends with two qualifications** ("consistent with thinner institutional capacity... though buyer size proxies for several correlated features"). The discipline is correct but the prose is heavier than ideal. Further compression would risk losing the qualifying clause that makes the gradient honest.

4. **Cobidder-vs-direct-defendant asymmetry** is now stated three times in the first 1.5 pages (abstract, second finding, scope). Defensible — the asymmetry is the design — but a referee may flag the repetition.
