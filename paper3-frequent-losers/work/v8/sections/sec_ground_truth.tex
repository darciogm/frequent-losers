\subsection{Ground Truth Robustness}
\label{sec:ground_truth}

Three tests distinguish market proximity from cover-bidder behavior.
\emph{First}, in CADE-present tenders, FL losing bids have a mean
log-spread of 3.08 vs.\ 1.84 for non-FL bids ($t = 12.1$,
$p < 0.001$)---FL firms' bids are drawn from a \emph{different
distribution} even within the same cartel-present tenders.
\emph{Second}, after conditioning on participation count, FL firms'
share of convicted-firm tenders is indistinguishable from non-FL
always-losers ($p = 0.93$), confirming that the unconditional
FL--CADE association reflects high participation volume; the
permutation test (3.5$\times$, Section~\ref{sec:cade}) already
controls for this.
\emph{Third}, CADE overlap concentrates \emph{entirely} among FL
firms: non-FL always-losers with cover-bid-like spreads have zero
CADE overlap, while FL firms without such spreads have 6.2\%
overlap---indicating that the participation anomaly, not the bid
pattern, drives the CADE association.
