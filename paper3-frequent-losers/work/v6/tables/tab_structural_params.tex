\begin{tablenotes}

\item \textit{Notes:} Two-stage maximum likelihood estimation of the cover-bid
mixture model (Section~\ref{sec:structural_estimation}). Panel~A reports the
genuine bid distribution estimated from non-FL losing bids with item and year
fixed effects. Panels~B--C report the cover-bid parameters under each regime,
estimated from FL bids conditional on the winning price. The 99th-percentile
log-spread $\hat{\delta}$ in Panel~B bounds the Regime~1 uniform support;
the median log-spread $\tilde{\delta} = 0.50$ indicates that the typical FL
bid exceeds the winning price by approximately 65\%
($e^{0.50} - 1 \approx 0.65$). Panel~D reports BIC model selection: Regime~2
(coordinated cover bidding) is strongly preferred ($\Delta\text{BIC} =
-91{,}473$). The ratio $\hat{\sigma}_c / \hat{\sigma}_g = 0.72$ indicates
that cover bids are less dispersed than genuine bids, consistent with
coordinated bid calibration near a focal point (Proposition~\ref{prop:regime2}).
Bootstrap standard errors from 100 tender-level replications.
