## Editorial repositioning

The manuscript was deeply rewritten to function as a **screening-and-enforcement paper**, not a mechanism paper. Every section now obeys the rule: "proposes a low-data screen, flags suspicious environments, does NOT identify cartel membership, does NOT causally identify the price effect, does NOT prove the mechanism."

## Sections fully rewritten

| Section | What changed |
|---------|-------------|
| **Abstract** | Restructured: enforcement need → FL screen → AUC/prices → complementarity → triage device. Added explicit "not a tool for establishing firm-level liability." |
| **Introduction** | Full rewrite. Opens with enforcement need. Framework = 2 subordinate sentences. Diagnostics = 1 compressed paragraph. Non-claims paragraph prominent. Scope paragraph added. |
| **Contributions (§1.1)** | Three contributions + non-claims paragraph. Non-claims says: not cartel membership, not causal, not mechanism proof, not sanction-grade evidence. |
| **Mechanisms (§8)** | Deep rewrite. Opener: "not sufficient for enforcement agency to act on." Joint assessment: "do not claim uniquely identifies...without substituting for forensic investigation." |

| **Limitations (§10)** | Rewritten as constitutional section. New "Diagnostics are not identification" subsection. New "Screen scope" framing. |
