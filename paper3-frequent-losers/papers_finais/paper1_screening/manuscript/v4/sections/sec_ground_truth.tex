\subsection{Ground Truth Robustness}
\label{sec:ground_truth}

Three checks assess whether the FL--CADE link reflects a behavioral
pattern rather than mere proximity. First, FL bids in CADE-present tenders have mean
log-spread 3.08 above the winner versus 1.84 for non-FL ($p < 0.001$).
Second, conditioning on participation count renders FL and non-FL
CADE overlap indistinguishable ($p = 0.93$)---the link reflects high
participation, which the permutation test already controls for.
Third, CADE overlap concentrates entirely among FL firms: non-FL
always-losers with cover-bid-like spreads show zero overlap. It is the participation anomaly, not the bid pattern, that drives
the association---consistent with the screen's design as a
participation-based triage tool.
