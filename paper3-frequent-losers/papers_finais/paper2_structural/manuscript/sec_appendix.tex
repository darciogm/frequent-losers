\section{Staggered Difference-in-Differences}
\label{app:did}

We construct a market-level panel (item $\times$ PBU $\times$ year)
where treatment is first FL entry. The sample is restricted to markets
with at least three pre- and three post-treatment observations, yielding
144,168 market-year observations across 19,777 markets (1,511 treated,
18,266 never-treated).

\paragraph{Rambachan--Roth sensitivity.}
We apply \cites{rambachan2023more} honest DiD bounds under smoothness
restrictions. At $\bar{M} = 0$ (parallel trends hold exactly), the
confidence interval is narrow; as $\bar{M}$ increases to allow for trend
deviations, the interval widens rapidly (Table~\ref{tab:did_revised},
Panel~C). With 80\% power, the MDE rises to
$(1.96 + 0.84) \times 0.039 = 0.109$.

\paragraph{Sharp-entry subsample.}
Restricting the treated sample to markets with ``sharp'' FL
entry---those where FL goes from zero to positive presence in a single
year---provides cleaner treatment timing. The C\&S ATT for this
subsample is reported in Panel~D.
