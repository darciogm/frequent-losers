results for $\log$ number of firms and $\log$ number of bids. FL-present
tenders have mechanically more total participants (by construction,
since FL firms are counted among participants) and approximately 35\%
more total bids. The economically meaningful comparison is the number of
\emph{genuine} (non-FL) firms, reported in Table~\ref{tab:nfirms_excl}
in the main text.
