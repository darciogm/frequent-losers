\noindent\textit{The Cost of Inclusion: Decomposing Bidder Exclusion in Public Procurement}

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}
\item SME set-aside raises winning prices 10--11\% in DiD against 76 controls.
\item 75\% of the simulated price effect is within-auction (sheltered bidding).
\item Endogenous SME entry attenuates a 50--60\% larger latent within-auction shock.
\item Welfare cost reaches 28.7\% (non-pharma) and 47.0\% (pharma) at $\lambda{=}0.30$.
\item A 10\% price preference welfare-dominates the set-aside in thick markets.
\end{itemize}
