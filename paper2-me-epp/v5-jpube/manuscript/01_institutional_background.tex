\section{Institutional Background}\label{sec:institutional}

Brazil's federal SME statute (Complementary Law 123/2006, subsequently amended by LC 147/2014 for procurement-rule provisions and LC 155/2016 for revenue-bracket adjustments) defines micro- and small enterprises by annual gross revenue ceilings of R\$360{,}000 (US\$103{,}000) and R\$4{,}800{,}000 (US\$1.37 million), respectively---the latter raised from R\$3.6M to R\$4.8M effective January 2018 by LC 155/2016---and mandates exclusive SME tenders for items valued at R\$80{,}000 (US\$22{,}900) or less. Public buyer units (PBUs)---federal, state, and municipal administrative entities subject to procurement rules---can avoid the SME-only default only by justifying the departure through a formal report to the audit courts, a costly and scrutinized process. LC 147/2014 converted what had been an option (``may'') into a binding default (``must'') and imposed these \emph{opt-out costs}; state-level adherence to SME-only tendering for eligible items rose from roughly 13\% to nearly 70\% in the subsequent years.\footnote{Adoption-rate figures and Group-65 sub-rates reported throughout this section are computed by the author from the BEC microdata extract used for the structural analysis (Section~\ref{sec:data}), restricted to items satisfying the federal SME-eligibility threshold of R\$80{,}000. The R\$4.8M EPP revenue ceiling raised by LC 155/2016 has no direct bite on the structural sample, where SME status is identified from the BEC administrative \emph{enquadramento} flag and the Receita Federal \emph{porte} register at the firm-auction level (Section~\ref{sec:data}); the bracket change is reported here for institutional completeness.}

Since September 2000 (Decree 45.085/2000, renamed by Decree 45.695/2001), the state of S\~ao Paulo has operated the Bolsa Eletr\^onica de Compras (BEC), a centralized electronic procurement platform; the Preg\~ao electronic reverse-auction format that anchors this paper's analytical sample was regulated within BEC by Decree 49.722/2005. BEC centralizes Preg\~ao for standardized-goods procurement by state bureaus and affiliated units, with registered suppliers submitting sealed bids (phase 1) followed by an iterative electronic auction (phases 2 onward) in which each participant may progressively lower its offer until the auction closes.\footnote{Brazilian institutional terms used throughout the paper (Preg\~ao, Convite, BEC, CADMAT, CMED, PGE-SP, TCE-SP, Sefaz-SP) are defined in Appendix~\ref{app:glossary}.} BEC handled approximately R\$13 billion (US\$3.7 billion) of procurement in 2019 alone and has cumulatively moved more than R\$105 billion (US\$30 billion) since implementation, spanning 860{,}000 purchase offers and 4.8 million item-level transactions.\footnote{Platform totals are reported by the Secretaria da Fazenda e Planejamento do Estado de S\~ao Paulo (Sefaz-SP) and the BEC operating unit at \url{www.bec.sp.gov.br/BECSP/Quem_Somos/Quem_Somos_BEC_novo.aspx} (retrieved April 2026); the 4.8-million item-level count and the 860{,}000 purchase-offer count are author tallies from the same BEC extract used in the structural analysis. \textbf{[VERIFY: monetary platform totals to be cross-checked against the official Sefaz-SP annual procurement bulletin URL before submission.]}} BEC organizes its catalog into three tiers---group, class, and item---so that ``medical, dental, and hospital equipment and supplies'' form Group 65, within which class 6531 identifies medications subject to price ceilings set by the C\^amara de Regula\c{c}\~ao do Mercado de Medicamentos (CMED).

Group 65 was a singular exception to the 2014 rule, but for a transversal legal reason rather than a sector-specific one. Until late 2017, the prevailing interpretation of art.~48,~I of LC 123/2006---both at the Tribunal de Contas do Estado de S\~ao Paulo (TCE-SP) and operationally inside BEC---was that the R\$80{,}000 threshold for SME-only tenders applied to the \emph{total estimated value of the procurement notice}, not to each individual item or lot. Under this restrictive reading, no notice with total estimated value above R\$80{,}000 could be made SME-only, regardless of whether individual items sat below the threshold. Group-65 procurement notices systematically combine small individual items (medications, supplies, dressings) with large notice totals reflecting bulk public-sector purchasing, so the restrictive interpretation mechanically barred Group 65 from the SME-only default---not because medical supplies were classified as ``strategic,'' but because their typical procurement structure (small unit values, large notice totals) was exactly what the totalizing interpretation excluded. The TCE-SP Tribunal Pleno had sustained this restrictive reading by tie-breaking vote on 23 September 2015 (eTC-5509.989.15), holding that ``the expression `items of contracting' refers to the entirety of the object sought by the Administration, and the sum of these values cannot exceed R\$80{,}000.''\footnote{TCE-SP, eTC-5509.989.15, Tribunal Pleno, sess\~ao de 23/09/2015 (decis\~ao por voto de desempate). Decisão acessível no portal de jurisprudência do TCE-SP (\url{https://www.tce.sp.gov.br/jurisprudencia}); a interpretação restritiva do art.~48,~I LC 123/2006 que prevaleceu nesse julgamento é amplamente discutida na literatura técnico-jur\'idica brasileira sobre licita\c{c}\~oes para ME/EPP.}

On 11 December 2017, the Procuradoria-Geral do Estado de S\~ao Paulo (PGE-SP) issued \textbf{Parecer Sub-G Cons.\ n\textordmasculine~151/2017}, doing two things at once. First, it held that the R\$80{,}000 threshold applies item-by-item, allowing mixed-exclusivity procurement notices in which items below R\$80{,}000 are SME-only and items above remain open. Second, it ruled that the more generous federal regime introduced by LC 147/2014 supersedes the previously applicable S\~ao Paulo state acts (\emph{Lei Estadual} 13.122/2008 and \emph{Decreto Estadual} 54.229/2009), which had embedded a less SME-favorable interpretation in state-level procurement practice. The combined holding is what destabilized the prevailing BEC operational practice. The argument was generic---about the legal interpretation of art.~48,~I and about federal-vs-state norm hierarchy---not specific to Group 65; the unlocking of Group-65 set-asides is a mechanical consequence of how medical-supplies notices are structured.\footnote{Parecer SubG-Cons.\ n\textordmasculine~151/2017, Subprocuradoria-Geral de Consultoria da PGE-SP, ementa: \emph{``Licita\c{c}\~oes exclusivas para ME e EPP (artigo 48, I, LC n\textordmasculine~123/2006) devem ser realizadas para itens ou lotes cujo valor estimado, individualmente, n\~ao ultrapasse o limite legal de R\$~80.000,00 (oitenta mil reais), \textbf{independentemente do valor total da Oferta de Compra}. \ldots\ Ap\'os a reforma operada pela Lei Complementar Federal n\textordmasculine~147/2014, a Lei Complementar Federal n\textordmasculine~123/2006 passou a destinar \`as MEs e EPPs tratamento simplificado e diferenciado em licita\c{c}\~oes e contrata\c{c}\~oes mais favor\'avel do que aquele previsto na Lei Estadual n\textordmasculine~13.122/2008 e no Decreto Estadual n\textordmasculine~54.229/2009. Aplicabilidade, na \'integra, da normativa federal.''} The opinion was submitted to the Procurador Geral do Estado for approval, after which it became binding guidance for state-level PBUs; the parecer is officially disclosed on the BEC/SP legislation portal (\url{https://www.bec.sp.gov.br/becsp/Legislacao/UI_Selecao.aspx}, \emph{Pareceres} section, retrieved April 2026). \textbf{[VERIFY: Boletim CEPGE volume/page reference and direct DOE-SP issue/page for the formal publication of the PGE approval to be inserted before submission.]}} Operationally, BEC moved before PGE consolidated the legal interpretation: \textbf{COMUNICADO BEC n\textordmasculine~02/2017 (18 July 2017)} introduced the SME-only OC functionality, and \textbf{COMUNICADO BEC n\textordmasculine~03/2017 (20 November 2017)} expanded it to mixed-exclusivity Pregão notices combining ample-participation items, 25\%-quota items, and SME-only items in the same notice---the precise operational mechanism that Parecer 151/2017 ratified three weeks later.\footnote{COMUNICADO BEC n\textordmasculine~02/2017 (18/07/2017): \emph{``Nova Funcionalidade -- gera\c{c}\~ao de OC com op\c{c}\~ao de participa\c{c}\~ao exclusiva ME/EPP/Cooperativa c/ Dir.\ Pref.''}; COMUNICADO BEC n\textordmasculine~03/2017 (20/11/2017): \emph{``Nova Funcionalidade -- gera\c{c}\~ao de Oferta de Compra -- OC -- Preg\~ao Eletr\^onico -- Material com op\c{c}\~oes de itens -- AMPLA, COTA at\'e 25\% para ME/EPP e Coop.\ c/ Dir.\ Pref.\ e exclusiva para ME/EPP e Coop.''} Both atos disclosed at the BEC/SP legislation portal (\url{https://www.bec.sp.gov.br/becsp/Legislacao/UI_Selecao.aspx}, se\c{c}\~ao Comunicados, retrieved April 2026).} TCE-SP did not immediately follow: under eTC-5509.989.15 the Tribunal Pleno had previously sustained the restrictive interpretation, and only reversed course on 16 May 2018 in eTC-9589.989.18, two and a half months after the empirical cutoff of this paper.\footnote{TCE-SP, eTC-9589.989.18, Tribunal Pleno, sess\~ao de 16/05/2018, alinhando-se ao Parecer Sub-G Cons.\ 151/2017 da PGE-SP quanto \`a interpreta\c{c}\~ao item-a-item do limite de R\$80~mil. Decis\~ao acess\'ivel no portal de jurisprud\^encia do TCE-SP (\url{https://www.tce.sp.gov.br/jurisprudencia}).} The empirical cutoff of March 2018 therefore captures the moment of mass take-up of the new functionality by Group-65 PBUs, not the moment of formal regulatory enablement (which was incremental between July and November 2017); in the months after the cutoff, SME-only adherence within Group 65 jumped to 43\% on average, below the rate for other groups largely because class-6531 pharmaceuticals are oligopolistic and often fail the three-eligible-SMEs requirement.

What matters for identification is that the policy change was triggered by a transversal legalistic reinterpretation of art.~48,~I at PGE-SP, not by anything happening in the Group-65 procurement market. Parecer SubG-Cons.\ 151/2017 made no substantive claim about medical supplies or any other product group; it pronounced on a general statutory question. The Group-65 unlocking that followed is the mechanical interaction of a generic threshold rule with the structural feature of medical procurement (small items, large notice totals). Public documentation turns up no sign that the parecer was a response to price levels, competition patterns, or supplier complaints specific to Group 65 in 2017; the timing was set by the bureaucratic process inside PGE-SP and by when BEC operationalized the new interpretation. The TCE-SP reversal in May 2018 lies after the empirical cutoff and confirms that the trigger was a top-down legal interpretation---when the audit court itself flipped, it ratified what PGE-SP had already established for state-level procurement, rather than initiating an independent change in the Group-65 environment. The change is therefore plausibly exogenous to the outcomes studied. This is the institutional basis for Assumption~\ref{a:setaside}: the cutoff shifts admissibility and equilibrium entry selection, not the underlying production technology of the goods themselves.
