\section{Appendix: Glossary of Brazilian Institutional Terms}\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}
\item[Preg\~ao] Electronic descending-clock English-reverse auction format defined by Federal Law 10.520/2002 and implemented on BEC for items above the small-value threshold. The clock opens at the buyer's reference price; participants submit downward-revisable offers until only one firm remains. Habilita\c{c}\~ao (qualification of the winning firm) follows the bidding stage. Preg\~ao is the operative auction format for the structural analysis of this paper (Section~\ref{sec:model}, Assumption~\ref{a:ipv}).

\item[Convite] Restricted-invitation sealed-bid tender for sub-threshold items, defined by Federal Law 8.666/1993 and reorganized under Federal Law \leiNova. The buyer invites at least three pre-registered suppliers, who submit sealed bids in a single round. Convite is used in this paper as the cross-modality validation sample (Section~\ref{sec:identification}, Table~\ref{tab:v3_cross_modality}).

\item[BEC] \emph{Bolsa Eletr\^onica de Compras} (electronic procurement exchange), the S\~ao Paulo state procurement platform, in operation since 2005 and centralizing all standardized-goods procurement for state bureaus and affiliated units.

\item[CADMAT] The BEC product catalog hierarchy: \emph{group} (e.g., Group 65 = medical, dental, hospital supplies) $\to$ \emph{class} (e.g., 6531 = CMED-regulated medications) $\to$ \emph{item} (specific procurement product code). The structural sample is defined at the (group, class, item) level.

\item[CMED] \emph{C\^amara de Regula\c{c}\~ao do Mercado de Medicamentos} (Drug Market Regulation Chamber), the federal pharmaceutical price-cap regulator. CMED-regulated medications form CADMAT class 6531 in the structural sample.

\item[PGE-SP] \emph{Procuradoria-Geral do Estado de S\~ao Paulo} (S\~ao Paulo state attorney general's office), the source of the \policyCutoffMonth{} legal opinion that extended the SME-only rule to medical and hospital supplies. The exogeneity argument of the paper rests on the bureaucratic timing of the PGE-SP opinion (Section~\ref{sec:institutional}).

\item[TCE-SP] \emph{Tribunal de Contas do Estado de S\~ao Paulo} (S\~ao Paulo state audit court), the audit body that, jointly with the state government, operated the pre-2018 Group-65 exception under which medical supplies were exempt from the SME-only default.

\item[Sefaz-SP] \emph{Secretaria da Fazenda e Planejamento do Estado de S\~ao Paulo} (S\~ao Paulo state finance and planning ministry), BEC's parent agency. Platform totals reported in Section~\ref{sec:institutional} are sourced from Sefaz-SP and the BEC operating unit.
\end{description}

\section{Appendix: Robustness}\label{app:robust}

The following tables support Section~\ref{sec:robustness}. Table~\ref{tab:v3_turnbull_fc} compares the Turnbull NPMLE CDF against losers-only and all-bidders at the median and upper quartile, documenting the ordering Turnbull $\le$ all-bidders $\le$ losers-only at the median in all 8 strata and at the 75th percentile in 6 of 8 strata (the two pharma non-SME cells reverse, with Turnbull marginally below all-bidders). Table~\ref{tab:v3_sensitivity_fc} runs the BNE decomposition under the three regimes and confirms the headline within \turnbullDeviationPct~percent of the total effect across classes. Table~\ref{tab:v3_strict_invariance} reports the strict-primitive-invariance benchmark, which preserves the intensive-margin result while changing the pharma policy ranking. Table~\ref{tab:v3_bandwidth_grid} varies the GPV kernel bandwidth by the factor grid \bandwidthGrid{} and reports the induced variation in $c_{0.5}$ and $c_{0.75}$. Table~\ref{tab:v3_bootstrap_ci} reports the 95 percent confidence intervals for the total effect, the intensive share, and the entry share, from a cluster bootstrap at the auction level with $\bootReps$ replicates. Table~\ref{tab:v3_filter_sensitivity} compares the baseline bid filter against tight, very-tight, and loose alternatives. Table~\ref{tab:v3_window_sensitivity} compares the 18-month-each-side window (the structural baseline) against 12 and 6 months on each side.
