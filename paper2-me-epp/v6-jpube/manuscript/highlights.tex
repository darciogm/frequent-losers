\noindent\textit{Sheltered Bidding: The Within-Auction Cost of SME Set-Asides}

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}
\item SME-only set-aside imposes R\$\,55--128M (US\$\,16--37M) in annual welfare loss on S\~ao Paulo's medical-supplies procurement alone.
\item Roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of the cost is sheltered bidding within the auction, not entry-margin reshuffling.
\item Endogenous SME entry is a partial offset that attenuates a 59\% larger latent within-auction shock in non-pharma and an 81\% larger one in pharma.
\item DiD against 76 never-treated controls: 10--11\% rise in winning prices; SME participation roughly doubles.
\item A 10\% SME price preference welfare-dominates the set-aside in thick standardized markets at near-zero fiscal cost.
\item The \emph{empate ficto} right-to-match (LC 123/2006 arts.~44--45) operative in current Brazilian law sits below the price preference on both distributive and welfare margins: \(\sim\)1--2 pp SME win-rate gain at essentially zero welfare cost.
\end{itemize}
