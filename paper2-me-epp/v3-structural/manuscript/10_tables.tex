\section{Appendix: Robustness}\label{app:robust}

The following tables support Section~\ref{sec:robustness}. Table~\ref{tab:v3_turnbull_fc} compares the Turnbull NPMLE CDF against losers-only and all-bidders at the median and upper quartile, documenting the strict ordering $F^{\text{Turnbull}} > F^{\text{all}} > F^{\text{losers}}$ at every quantile. Table~\ref{tab:v3_sensitivity_fc} runs the BNE decomposition under the three regimes and confirms the headline within 5 percent. Table~\ref{tab:v3_strict_invariance} reports the strict-primitive-invariance benchmark, which preserves the intensive-margin result while changing the pharma policy ranking. Table~\ref{tab:v3_bandwidth_grid} varies the GPV kernel bandwidth by factors of 0.5 through 2.0 and reports the induced variation in $c_{0.5}$ and $c_{0.75}$. Table~\ref{tab:v3_bootstrap_ci} reports the 95 percent confidence intervals for the total effect, the intensive share, and the entry share, from a cluster bootstrap at the auction level with B=500 replicates. Table~\ref{tab:v3_filter_sensitivity} compares the baseline bid filter against tight, very-tight, and loose alternatives. Table~\ref{tab:v3_window_sensitivity} compares the 18-month window against 12 and 6 months.

\section{Appendix: Supplementary Figures}\label{app:figures}

The following figures support the identification and diagnostic checks discussed in Sections~\ref{sec:identification} through~\ref{sec:robustness}. Figure~\ref{fig:v3_cross_modality_raw} plots the raw (pre-UH) Convite GPV versus Preg\~ao drop-out comparison---the uncorrected counterpart to main-text Figure~\ref{fig:v3_cross_modality_uh}, illustrating how much of the cross-modality gap the UH correction absorbs. Figure~\ref{fig:v3_pregao_fc_by_stratum} plots the Preg\~ao drop-out point-identified $F_c^k$ by stratum. Figure~\ref{fig:v3_pregao_ht_bands} overlays the Haile-Tamer upper-bound refinement on the same estimates, documenting that the point-ID recoveries lie within the Haile-Tamer bounds. Figure~\ref{fig:v3_bne_prices} plots the simulated BNE price ECDFs by counterfactual scenario, the distributional counterpart to the means reported in main-text Table~\ref{tab:v3_bne_decomp}.
