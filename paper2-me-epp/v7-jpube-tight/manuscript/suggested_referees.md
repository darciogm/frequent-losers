# Suggested Referees — *The Cost of Inclusion*

## Positive list (4 names)

1. **Elena Krasnokutskaya** (Johns Hopkins University)
   *Why:* Anchor reference of the partial-preference structural-procurement literature this paper extends to total exclusion. Her Krasnokutskaya (2011) auction-level UH shrinkage is used in the paper. Best-positioned referee to evaluate identification and decomposition rigor.

2. **Francesco Decarolis** (Bocconi University)
   *Why:* Editor of *Journal of Industrial Economics*, JPubE-adjacent author on procurement design, corruption, and emerging-market procurement (Decarolis et al. 2024 cited in the paper). Strong on policy-design framing.

3. **Susan Athey** (Stanford University)
   *Why:* Coauthor of Athey-Levin-Seira (2011), an anchor reference for the entry treatment. Susan has been historically receptive to applied-procurement work and her co-authorship on that paper makes her the strongest second-tier match for assessing the entry-equilibrium framing.

4. **Stephane Bonhomme** (University of Chicago)
   *Why:* Auction econometrics, Haile-Tamer extensions, identification-conscious referee. Provides external balance to the policy-leaning referees above.

## Alternates (in case any of the above declines)

5. **Karam Kang** (University of Notre Dame) — emerging-market procurement, structural empirical IO.
6. **Daniel Quint** (University of Wisconsin) — auction theory, procurement preferences.
7. **Dimitri Szerman** (PUC-Rio) — Brazilian procurement, firm-level effects of public purchases (Ferraz, Finan, Szerman 2016 and Szerman 2023 cited in the paper).
8. **Vitor Possebom** (Insper) — econometric identification, emerging-market natural experiments.

## Exclusion list (2 names)

1. **Paulo Furquim de Azevedo** (Insper) — thanked in the manuscript footnote for early discussions; would have a conflict of interest as referee.

2. **[Reserved]** — author currently has no second exclusion to declare. If the editor consults a referee with a known prior dispute or a direct collaboration tie that is not visible in the paper's acknowledgements, the editor is invited to use her judgment.

## Notes for the editor

- The paper is sole-authored. There is no co-author to reach out to for additional referee suggestions.
- The Brazilian setting may benefit from at least one referee with regional expertise (Cabral, Possebom) but the methodology is not regional and the principal evaluation should be on the structural-econometric and welfare-arithmetic merits.
- A balanced referee panel ideally pairs one structural-IO referee (Krasnokutskaya, Athey, or Bonhomme) with one public-economics / procurement-policy referee (Decarolis or Cabral). The author trusts editorial judgment on the precise pairing.
