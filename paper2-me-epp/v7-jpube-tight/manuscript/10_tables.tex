\section{Appendix: Glossary of Brazilian Institutional Terms}\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}
\item[Preg\~ao] Electronic descending-clock English-reverse auction (Federal Law 10.520/2002), implemented on BEC for items above the small-value threshold; the operative auction format of this paper (Section~\ref{sec:model}).
\item[Convite] Restricted-invitation sealed-bid tender for sub-threshold items (Federal Law 8.666/1993, reorganized under \leiNova); used here as the cross-modality validation sample (Section~\ref{sec:identification}).
\item[BEC] \emph{Bolsa Eletr\^onica de Compras}, the S\~ao Paulo state procurement platform (since 2005), centralizing standardized-goods procurement for state bureaus and affiliated units.
\item[CADMAT] BEC product catalog hierarchy: \emph{group} (e.g., 65 = medical/dental/hospital supplies) $\to$ \emph{class} (e.g., 6531 = CMED-regulated medications) $\to$ \emph{item}.
\item[CMED] \emph{C\^amara de Regula\c{c}\~ao do Mercado de Medicamentos}, the federal pharmaceutical price-cap regulator; CMED-regulated medications = CADMAT class 6531.
\item[PGE-SP] \emph{Procuradoria-Geral do Estado de S\~ao Paulo} (state attorney general); source of the \policyCutoffMonth{} legal opinion that extended SME-only to medical supplies.
\item[TCE-SP] \emph{Tribunal de Contas do Estado de S\~ao Paulo} (state audit court); operated the pre-2018 Group-65 exception jointly with the state government.
\item[Sefaz-SP] \emph{Secretaria da Fazenda e Planejamento}, BEC's parent agency; source of platform totals reported in Section~\ref{sec:institutional}.
\end{description}

\section{Appendix: Identification Diagnostics}\label{app:id}

Seven tables support the identifying-assumption diagnostics of Sections~\ref{sec:model}--\ref{sec:identification}. Table~\ref{tab:v3_primitive_invariance} reports the KS test of Assumption~\ref{a:setaside}; the non-rejection in pharma Convite non-SMEs ($D=\ksConvitePharmaNS$) is the tightest validation. Table~\ref{tab:v3_cross_modality} compares Convite GPV and Preg\~ao drop-out recoveries, coinciding within two pp at the median in pharma non-SME Pre. Tables~\ref{tab:v3_uh_variance}--\ref{tab:v3_uh_vs_raw} report the auction-level variance decomposition and the shrinkage comparison. Table~\ref{tab:v3_uh_invariance} re-runs the KS test on UH-clean bids: the Convite pharma non-SME stratum still satisfies $D < \ksDistThresh$ ($D = \ksUHinvCheckLo$), the Preg\~ao pharma stratum continues to reject ($D = \ksPharmaPregao \to \ksPharmaPregaoUH$), and the Preg\~ao non-pharma stratum crosses to $D = \ksNpPregaoUH$---i.e.\ its apparent raw-test invariance reflected the common scale, not the firm-level primitive. Tables~\ref{tab:v3_pregao_fc_summary}--\ref{tab:v3_pregao_ht_refined} summarize the Preg\~ao drop-out estimator and the Haile--Tamer refinement.
