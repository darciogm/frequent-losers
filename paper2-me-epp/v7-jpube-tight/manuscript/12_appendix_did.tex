\section{Appendix: Reduced-Form Benchmark}\label{app:did}

The structural exercise of Sections~\ref{sec:results}--\ref{sec:welfare} is anchored to a magnitude in the data: the absolute log-price effect of the \policyCutoffMonth{} cutoff. This appendix reports that magnitude as a difference-in-differences against the \controlGroupCount{} never-treated product groups, on the same sample window used by the structural exercise. The DiD is included for design validation; the structural exercise interprets the within-auction and participation-margin channels that this number aggregates.

The estimating equation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:did}
\log p^{\mathrm{final}}_{i,j,t} \;=\; \alpha_i + \mu_t + \beta \, (\mathrm{g65}_j \cdot \text{Pre}_t) + X'_{i,j,t} \gamma + \varepsilon_{i,j,t},
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_i$ is an item fixed effect, $\mu_t$ a calendar-month fixed effect, $X_{i,j,t}$ collects auction-format and quantity controls, and $\beta$ identifies the open-regime price level relative to the post-cutoff level in the difference-in-differences-in-reverse sense of \citet{kim2019}: a negative coefficient on the pre-period dummy is the open-regime discount that the SME-only rule undoes after \policyCutoffMonth.

Table~\ref{tab:prices} reports $\hat\beta$ across 6-, 12-, and 18-month windows. The \structuralWindowM-month specification (the structural baseline) gives $\hat\beta = \didPriceEighteen$ without and $\didPriceEighteenPbu$ with PBU controls, both significant at 1 percent on item-clustered standard errors. The 12- and 6-month windows give similar estimates ($\didPriceTwelve$ and $\didPriceSix$); the 6-month coefficient captures the immediate post-cutoff price response, the 12- and 18-month coefficients reflect progressive absorption of the within-auction shock by SME entry. The \didLowerPct--\didUpperPct~percent magnitude anchoring the structural calibration is the \structuralWindowM-month log-price coefficient. Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} reports the same specification with $p^{\mathrm{final}}/p^{\mathrm{ref}}$ as the outcome on a level scale; the 6-month window gives $\didEffSix$ (with PBU controls), consistent with the log-price effect adjusted for the average pre-period ratio $\didPreRefRatio$.

\subsection{Event-study and placebo diagnostics}

Figure~\ref{fig:eventstudy_prices} plots $\hat\beta_s$ for six semesters around the cutoff with item and semester fixed effects. Pre-cutoff coefficients are small in magnitude relative to the post-cutoff coefficients; the post-cutoff semesters align in direction and magnitude with the $\didLowerDec$ to $\didUpperDec$ headline. Table~\ref{tab:placebo} disciplines the cutoff timing against three placebo treatment dates (September 2017, March 2017, June 2017), with placebo coefficients of $\placeboSeventeenSep/\placeboSeventeenMar/\placeboSeventeenJun$. The relevant test for a structural-calibration anchor is magnitude separation, not absence of pre-trend: the actual \policyCutoffMonth{} coefficient ($\placeboLowerNp$ to $\placeboHigherNp$) is three to eight times larger in absolute value than any placebo coefficient and significant at the 1 percent level in every specification. Per \citet{borusyak2024, sun2021} on event-study contamination, the magnitude-separation bound on the anticipation contribution is at least \placeboMagnitudeRatio~percent of the real-cutoff magnitude---well within the band a structural-calibration anchor can absorb.
