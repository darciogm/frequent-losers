\section{Appendix: External Validity---Global Catalogue of Bidder-Exclusion and Preference Rules}\label{app:external}

Table~\ref{tab:v3_external_policies} organizes a representative set of identity-based procurement rules by the mechanism through which they restrict the bidder pool. Rules in Block~A (full exclusion) are those to which the paper's sheltered-bidding decomposition applies without additional parametric structure; rules in Block~B (price or score preference) involve the intensive--extensive entanglement that the partial-preference literature addresses with parametric bid-function inversion; rules in Block~C operate through channels other than per-auction bidder eligibility. The table supports the discussion of external validity in Section~\ref{sec:external}.
