\section*{Appendix: Robustness Checks}
\label{sec:appendix}

This appendix presents robustness checks for the main results across alternative winsorization levels: no winsorization (Panel~A), 1\%/99\% baseline (Panel~B), and 5\%/95\% aggressive winsorization (Panel~C). Additionally, we report robustness of the ``under the gun'' estimates to progressive inclusion of control variables.

\subsection*{A.1 Winsorization Sensitivity}

Tables~\ref{tab:rob_ref_prices}--\ref{tab:rob_success} show that the main results are robust to the choice of winsorization level. Coefficients are qualitatively stable across all three treatments, with modest quantitative variation. The baseline 1\%/99\% winsorization (Panel~B) provides our preferred estimates.

\subsection*{A.2 Under the Gun: Progressive Controls}

Table~\ref{tab:rob_utg} examines the sensitivity of the ``under the gun'' estimate to progressive inclusion of controls (log quantity, log reference price, log number of firms) and to the choice of time fixed effects. The coefficient on the administrative indicator remains negative and significant (or close to significant) across specifications and winsorization levels, confirming the robustness of the sanction channel finding.
