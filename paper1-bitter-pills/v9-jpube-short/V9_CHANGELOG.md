# V9 Changelog: JPubE Short Paper

## Main Paper

Version directory: `v9-jpube-short/`

Main file: `manuscript/paper/main.tex`

Compiled PDF: `manuscript/paper/main.pdf`

Title: “Sourcing under Sanctions: Judicial Urgency and Pharmaceutical Procurement Costs”

Structure:
- Introduction
- Institutional Background
- Data, Classification, and Samples
- Empirical Framework
- Results
- Falsification and Robustness
- Policy Interpretation and Limits

Key change from v8 source material:
- Reframed the contribution around pricing versus sourcing under court-mandated urgent procurement.
- Moved Manski-Lee selection bounds and within firm-buyer-item evidence into the main paper.
- Reframed the within-firm result as a deep-market null, with markups reappearing in thinner and earlier subsamples.
- Removed full-length-paper framing and generic judicialization claims.
